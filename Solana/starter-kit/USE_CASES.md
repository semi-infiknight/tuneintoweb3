# Solana Real-World Use Cases

Solana is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **DeFi Platforms**: Lending, swaps, and stablecoins (e.g., Mango Markets, Serum)
- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs (e.g., Magic Eden)
- **Gaming**: On-chain assets and logic (e.g., Star Atlas)
- **DAOs**: Community governance and treasury management
- **Payments**: Fast, low-fee transactions

## Example Project: Simple Counter
- The included template demonstrates deploying and interacting with a simple counter program on Solana using Anchor.

# Apechain Real-World Use Cases

Apechain is used in a variety of decentralized applications, especially in gaming and NFTs:

- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs with low fees
- **Blockchain Games**: In-game assets as NFTs, play-to-earn mechanics
- **Metaverse Projects**: Virtual worlds with NFT-based land and items
- **DAOs**: Community governance for gaming guilds
- **Cross-chain Gaming**: Interoperable assets across games

## Example Project: Simple NFT
- The included template demonstrates deploying and interacting with an ERC721 NFT contract on Apechain.
- Extend it to build NFT marketplaces, games, or metaverse dApps!

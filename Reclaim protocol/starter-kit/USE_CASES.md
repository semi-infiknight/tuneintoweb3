# Reclaim Protocol Real-World Use Cases

Reclaim Protocol is used in a variety of privacy-preserving applications, including:

- **Credential Verification**: Prove ownership of social accounts, email, or other credentials without revealing sensitive data
- **On-chain Attestations**: Issue verifiable credentials for DAOs, DeFi, or NFT projects
- **Sybil Resistance**: Prevent bots and fake accounts in Web3 communities
- **Private Access Control**: Gate access to dApps or content based on verified credentials

## Example Project: Social Account Verification
- The included template demonstrates verifying a social account credential using Reclaim Protocol SDK.
- Extend it to build private access, on-chain attestations, or Sybil resistance solutions!

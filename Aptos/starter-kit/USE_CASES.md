# Aptos Real-World Use Cases

Aptos is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **DeFi Platforms**: Lending, swaps, and stablecoins using Move modules
- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs with Move
- **Gaming**: On-chain assets and logic in Move
- **DAOs**: Community governance and treasury management
- **Payments**: Fast, low-fee transactions

## Example Project: Simple Coin
- The included template demonstrates deploying and interacting with a custom Move coin on Aptos.
- Extend it to build DeFi, NFT, or gaming dApps!

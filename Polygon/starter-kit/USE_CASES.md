# Polygon Real-World Use Cases

Polygon is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **DeFi Platforms**: Fast, low-cost swaps and lending (e.g., Aave, Quickswap)
- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs with low fees (e.g., OpenSea, Zed Run)
- **Gaming**: Blockchain-based games with in-game assets (e.g., Decentral Games)
- **DAOs**: Community governance and treasury management
- **Payments**: Micropayments and remittances

# Monad Real-World Use Cases

Monad is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **DeFi Platforms**: Fast, low-cost swaps and lending
- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs with low fees
- **Gaming**: Blockchain-based games with in-game assets
- **DAOs**: Community governance and treasury management
- **Enterprise**: Custom EVM-compatible solutions

## Example Project: Simple Token
- The included template demonstrates deploying and interacting with an ERC20 token on Monad.
- Extend it to build DeFi, NFT, or gaming dApps!

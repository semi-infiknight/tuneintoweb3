# Stellar Real-World Use Cases

Stellar is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **Payments**: Fast, low-fee cross-border payments
- **Asset Issuance**: Stablecoins, tokenized assets, loyalty points
- **Remittances**: Global money transfers
- **Micropayments**: Streaming payments, pay-per-use APIs
- **NGO Aid Distribution**: Transparent, trackable disbursements

## Example Project: Custom Token
- The included template demonstrates issuing and transferring a custom asset on Stellar testnet.

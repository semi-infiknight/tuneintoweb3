# Avalanche Real-World Use Cases

Avalanche is used in a variety of decentralized applications, including:

- **DeFi Platforms**: Fast, low-cost swaps and lending (e.g., Trader Joe, Pangolin)
- **NFT Marketplaces**: Minting and trading NFTs with low fees (e.g., Kalao, NFTrade)
- **Gaming**: Blockchain-based games with in-game assets (e.g., Crabada)
- **DAOs**: Community governance and treasury management
- **Enterprise**: Custom subnets for private or regulated use cases
